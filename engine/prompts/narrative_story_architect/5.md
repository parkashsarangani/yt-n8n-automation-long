## Story shape

Use `intent.genre` when present, otherwise moral_story. Echo the same value in output `genre`.

- moral_story: parable; lesson earned through events, not a lecture.
- drama: conflict immediately; heightened but recognizably human stakes; explicit realization at the end is allowed.
- true_story: restrained, concrete, plausible incident; may end with an unresolved judgment question.
- short_story: literary turn/twist/irony; no stated moral.

The hook is the strongest beat in the episode. If the low point, reversal, or payoff would make a better cold open, restructure so the strongest concrete moment is scene 0 and later beats satisfy its promise rather than outshining it.

Build 3-5 acts around:
1. cold open already mid-action;
2. escalation that makes the opening stranger/worse/more consequential;
3. a genuine low point where the intuitive outcome looks bad;
4. turn/reversal/reveal;
5. payoff that resolves the exact question created by the package.

Every act must change the situation. Prefer BUT / THEREFORE causality over AND THEN chronology.

## Emotional engine

Build toward `growth.emotional_engine`. For the learning-stage channel, injustice/underestimation -> escalation -> lowest point -> competence/truth/sacrifice revealed -> reversal/vindication is the default audience promise when it actually fits the selected package. Do not force it when the package says otherwise.

## Duration

Honor `intent.target_duration_sec`. Aim roughly 130-150 spoken words/minute across all acts. Do not pad a short story or compress a story whose turn needs room.

## Ending and session continuation

`payoff` must satisfy the package promise and make the opening feel earned in hindsight.

`comment_hook` should invite a specific reaction/verdict when natural.

`outro_line` should protect the payoff. Prefer a natural continuation bridge based on `growth.next_video_bridge` when present. Do NOT default to a generic spoken like/subscribe CTA. A subscription ask is allowed only if it fits in one short natural breath without weakening the story ending.

## Output requirements

Return the normal story artifact fields: topic, title, hook, genre, acts with act_index/act_title/premise/target_words, payoff, optional comment_hook, and outro_line.

Confidence dimensions:
- hook_strength: low if anything later is a stronger cold open or the hook needs explanation to make sense.
- escalation_strength: low if the middle only adds context instead of changing stakes.
- payoff_strength: low if the ending does not resolve the selected click promise.
- package_fidelity: low if title/thumbnail/opening promised one story and this outline drifts into another.
