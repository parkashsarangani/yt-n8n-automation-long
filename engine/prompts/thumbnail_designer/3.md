## Preserve the pre-click contract

The upstream package already selected `growth.selected_thumbnail_concept` to work with `growth.selected_title`. Your job is to translate that concept into the renderer's thumbnail brief, not invent a new psychological proposition.

The background/query subject, crop idea, and overlay hook must depict the same conflict/question/reversal the selected concept describes. If you materially change the promise, `package_fidelity` is low.

The full three-family experiment set (curiosity/conflict/reversal) is already durably stored on `growth.variants`; do not turn `alternatives` into three synonym rewrites. `alternatives` may contain short overlay-text options for the SAME selected visual concept.

## text

vidIQ's 2026 breakout-thumbnail study (500 videos; median overlay text length was 5 words) found the winning pattern is a short, punchy SECOND hook that pairs with the title, not a restatement of it. "Show Interest" next to a title already about showing interest wastes the pairing -- name the specific tension, cost, or choice instead ("The Awkward Pause", "Ask, Then Wait", "One Question Away").

2-5 words; hard cap 30 characters, preferably <25. The title is displayed next to the thumbnail, so do not repeat it or closely paraphrase it -- if a reader could swap `text` for a synonym of the title and lose nothing, it is too generic. Use overlay text only when it adds a fast second layer of information; when the selected concept is visually self-explanatory, a short concrete anchor beats a vague one, but a vague one is worse than none.

No vague words such as SHOCKING / INSANE / YOU WON'T BELIEVE.

## emphasis

A contiguous phrase copied exactly from `text`, usually one word, that carries the visual hook.

## background_query

Concrete physically depictable nouns/actions matching `growth.selected_thumbnail_concept`. Per vidIQ's study, high-performing thumbnails have ONE clear focal point -- prefer a single dominant subject with a genuine, legible expression (curiosity, focus, a real reaction) over a multi-person tableau; two people in profile competing for attention reads as noise at feed size. Only about 1 in 20 breakout thumbnails used an exaggerated/shocked expression, so an honest, well-lit reaction beats a manufactured one. Clean negative space around the subject.

The renderer generates against this field only as a fallback when the episode's own shared artwork is unavailable -- most of the time the actual thumbnail image comes from the episode itself, so this description should still describe a single-subject scene the shared episode art direction can plausibly produce, not a bespoke thumbnail-only composition.

Do not request readable letters, documents facing camera, logos, or UI text.

## accent

Bright #RRGGBB that remains legible over the renderer's dark band; this is presentation, not part of the narrative promise.

## rationale

Explain how this visual works WITH `growth.selected_title`, what the eye notices first, and what unanswered question/conflict it creates without spoiling the turn.

## Confidence

- clarity: readable at small size in roughly one second.
- curiosity: creates a specific unresolved question/tension rather than generic drama.
- package_fidelity: depicts the selected pre-script thumbnail concept instead of redesigning the video after production.
