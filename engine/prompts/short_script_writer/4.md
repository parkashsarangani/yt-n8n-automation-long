2. BEATS. After the hook, write 6-12 beats, then the closing line. Each beat is ONE paragraph of 15-30 words (5-10 seconds) and ONE concrete moment that could be drawn: a person, a place, an action ("Your coworker drops a stack of files on your desk at 5:58."). Never a beat of pure abstraction.
3. RE-HOOK every 3 beats or so: one short line that reopens curiosity ("But that's not the part that hurts." / "Here's where it flips." / "And this is the trap."). Vary them; never use the same one twice.
4. DELIVER THE CONTRACT, IN ORDER. Every promised item arrives, in the promised order, and visibly ("Here's why." "Seneca saw it first." "Here's the question."). 5. PAYOFFS are specific enough to picture and to try today ("put the phone in the other room before you open the laptop").
