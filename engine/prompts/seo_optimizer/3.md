## The selected package is not disposable

`growth.selected_title` + `growth.selected_thumbnail_concept` were chosen BEFORE scripting and the script was gated against that promise. Do not casually replace the title with a different psychological proposition just because another phrase is more searchable.

Use `growth.selected_title` as the default `title`. You may make a small clarity/search phrasing correction only when it preserves the same curiosity/conflict/reversal promise. Never turn a conflict title into a generic search title or vice versa if that changes why the viewer clicks.
If `growth.selected_title` reads as an editor's summary rather than something a viewer would type into search (no "how to", no named fear/mistake, no concrete situation a search bar would recognise), this is exactly the "small clarity/search phrasing correction" you are allowed to make: fold in the closest natural search phrase the episode actually earns without changing which family's promise it delivers. "Interest Without Pressure" becoming "How To Show Interest Without Being Weird" keeps the same curiosity promise while giving it a phrase people search; that is a correction, not a new proposition. Prefer this over leaving a title that upstream packaging already flagged as unsearchable.

`alternative_titles` should preserve the three materially different package families already stored in `growth.variants`. Prefer those titles rather than inventing synonym variants. The growth_package artifact is the authoritative full title/thumbnail experiment set; seo_metadata is the publish-facing selected listing.

## primary_keyword

Choose one natural phrase a real person might search that the finished episode genuinely addresses. Searchability supports packaging; it does not override it.

## description

First ~150 characters: a human-readable sentence that contains the primary keyword and reinforces the selected promise without giving away the turn.

Then 2-3 short paragraphs describing what the episode actually covers using concrete specifics from the narration. Close with the story's specific comment/verdict question when appropriate.

No invented chapters/timestamps.

## tags

5-15 concise tags, under YouTube's aggregate character budget. Prefer exact topic/phrase variants and a few broader terms. Do not stuff irrelevant tags.

## rationale

Explain the intended viewer, why the selected title/thumbnail proposition is honest, and what search phrase it can still capture. Do not claim analytics evidence unless it exists in the supplied artifacts.

## Confidence

- searchability: low if the primary keyword is unnatural or unrelated.
- honesty: low if title/description promise something the narration does not deliver.
- package_fidelity: low if your publish title changes the psychological reason to click relative to `growth.selected_title`.
