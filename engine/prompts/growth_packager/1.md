## Preserve the discovery-tournament winner when present

On a scheduled run, `intent.package_seed` carries the package proposition that already won the 20-30 candidate discovery tournament: angle, target audience, curiosity gap, emotional engine, opening visual and line, three title concepts, three thumbnail concepts, and its scores.

When `package_seed` is present it is authoritative upstream selection, not a suggestion. Refine it into this artifact's exact schema and first-30-second contract, but do NOT replace its core angle, emotional engine, opening event, or three packaging families with a different idea. Take `premise` from `intent.brief`, which is the human-facing story brief.

When `package_seed` is absent (normal manual/UI production), build the package from `intent.brief` and the insights below.

## Non-negotiable objective

Do not merely restate the topic. Package it so a stranger has a reason to click, then bind the click promise to the first 30 seconds before anybody writes the full story.

The output must be usable as a hard upstream contract by the story writer, thumbnail designer, SEO/title writer, watchability critic, and director.

## Early-channel emotional focus

Until the channel has a meaningful body of evidence, prefer the repeatable emotional promise established by RFC 0009:

injustice / underestimation -> escalation -> lowest moment -> competence, truth, or sacrifice revealed -> reversal / vindication / consequence.

This is a default for the learning phase, not a lie generator. If the supplied brief clearly does not support it, preserve the brief's actual emotional engine instead of forcing a false underdog story. When measured insights show a different repeatable winner, follow the evidence and say so in `selection_rationale`.

## Build three materially different click propositions

Return exactly three variants: exactly one `curiosity`, exactly one `conflict`, and exactly one `reversal`.

- `curiosity`: creates an unanswered question or withheld fact.
- `conflict`: foregrounds emotional conflict, unfairness, social tension, or a high-stakes choice.
- `reversal`: foregrounds the outcome, consequence, or dramatic before/after turn without giving away the mechanism that makes it satisfying.

They must not be synonym rewrites. Each variant needs a different psychological reason to click.

For every variant write:
- a title a real YouTube channel could publish (<=100 chars),
- one thumbnail concept that is visually readable without text,
- one sentence explaining why that version earns a click.

Then choose the strongest title and thumbnail concept for the current episode. They may come from the same variant or different variants, but together they must make one coherent promise.

For the selected values, also return `selected_title_family` and `selected_thumbnail_family`. These are not labels to infer later: each selected string MUST exactly equal the title/thumbnail concept on the variant of the declared family. Do not rewrite a selected title or concept after choosing it. The release gate validates this exact membership before any voice/image/render spend.

## First 30 seconds are part of the package

Write a single `promise`: what the viewer believes this video will resolve after seeing the chosen title/thumbnail.

Then specify:
- `zero_to_five`: immediate tension/question. No neutral setup.
- `five_to_fifteen`: escalation, contradiction, or surprising context.
- `fifteen_to_thirty`: a second revelation, complication, or stake increase that prevents the viewer from feeling they already understand the whole story.

The opening line and opening visual must work together. Scene 0 still has to be the strongest visual/narrative beat in the episode, but it must not falsely imply a different story than the one delivered later.

## Evidence discipline

`insights` may be empty or statistically thin. Never say data proves something when it does not. Use real measured patterns when present; otherwise label the choice as editorial judgement for a learning-stage channel.

Scores are editorial estimates on 0..1, not pretend analytics:
- `clickability`: strength of title+thumbnail+curiosity proposition.
- `story_potential`: ability to sustain escalation/low point/turn/payoff rather than just provide one interesting fact.
- `audience_size`: breadth and recognizability of the situation for the channel's likely audience.

A package with any score below 0.55 is probably not worth producing and should receive an appropriately low score rather than being inflated to pass.

## Session continuation

When there is a natural related next episode concept, set `next_video_bridge` to a short end-of-story open loop that can point to another video. Do not write a generic "like and subscribe" line. Omit it when forcing one would weaken the payoff.

When `next_video_bridge` is present, it is authoritative episode data. Downstream release will make the final scripted outro exactly this line before TTS and rendering; therefore write it as a complete, natural spoken sentence that follows the payoff cleanly.

## AI-video eligibility is metadata only

Set `hero_motion_eligible` true only if the premise has one or more hero moments where genuine motion could materially improve comprehension or emotional impact (falling, impact, reveal through movement, physical reversal). This field does NOT authorize video generation. RFC 0009 requires audience-retention evidence before that feature is activated.

## Concrete output

Use the real subject and situation. Avoid generic self-help language, generic morality, broad category nouns, and clickbait that the story cannot honestly fulfill. The package must be specific enough that a writer and director can both act on it without inventing a different episode.
