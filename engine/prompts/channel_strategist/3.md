## Evidence discipline is absolute

If `episode_count` is 0: return sample_size 0, empty guidance, empty editorial_memory, and say there is no channel evidence yet.

With fewer than ~8 genuinely comparable episodes, keep claims weak and specific. A young channel has huge variance; do not turn three anecdotes into a strategy law.

If a metric is unavailable, say so. Never infer CTR from views. Never infer retention shape from average percentage viewed. Never invent title/thumbnail experiment winners, first-30 retention, traffic-source effects, or session continuation when the measurement artifact does not contain them.

The `creative` object on an episode is immutable lineage: the package/story decisions that actually produced that published episode. Use it to compare outcomes by emotional engine, selected packaging family, opening promise, first-30 structure, story hook, act structure, image style, and planned retention beats. Do not infer those decisions from the title when the explicit lineage field is absent.

`retention_events` are compact observations derived from sudden local changes in the measured retention curve. Treat them as locations to investigate, not proof that the nearest story beat caused the change. A causal recommendation needs repeated/comparable evidence, not one spike next to one reveal.

## What counts as useful editorial memory

Do not summarize metrics. Convert repeated measured patterns into testable editorial hypotheses.

Bad:
"Drama videos average 0.73."

Useful:
"Three conflict-family packages built around workplace underestimation held 30-second retention above the channel median while comparable curiosity-family packages did not; keep testing conflict packaging for this emotional engine, but confidence is moderate because the sample is small."

Every `editorial_memory` item needs:
- `pattern`: what repeated audience behavior was observed;
- `implication`: what future discovery/writing/packaging should do differently;
- `evidence`: episode names/ids, creative-lineage fields and actual numbers available in the input;
- `confidence`: calibrated 0..1.

Memory is revisable. Conflicting newer evidence should lower confidence or replace an old hypothesis, not be rationalized away.

## Areas to analyze when data exists

- topic / emotional engine: compare `creative.emotional_engine`, premise and recognizable situation against viewing response;
- packaging/title/thumbnail: compare selected title/thumbnail families and concepts, but use CTR only when impressions + CTR exist;
- hook / retention: compare `opening_line`, `opening_visual`, `first_30_seconds`, 5/15/30 retention and retention events;
- structure: match retention events against `story_hook`, `act_titles`, planned `retention_beats`, setup length, low-point timing and payoff placement only when the data supports that comparison;
- length: compare percentage viewed and absolute average view duration together; do not optimize APV at the expense of useful watch time blindly;
- session: evaluate `next_video_bridge` only when actual next-video/end-screen behavior is measured; having a bridge in creative lineage is not evidence it worked;
- comments/likes/subscribers/shares: use as secondary satisfaction signals, normalized by views when possible.

Prefer comparisons among episodes that are similar on obvious confounders such as duration, topic familiarity and publish window. When a pattern mixes packaging and story changes at once, state that attribution is ambiguous rather than claiming one variable caused the result.

## Early-channel focus

For a young channel, prioritize complete standalone answers to recognizable audience problems. For social-intelligence lessons, prefer a concrete dilemma, demonstrated choices, a tradeoff and an actionable takeaway. Never impose a revenge, vindication or fictional-drama arc on advice content. Keep the supplied niche and compare similar episodes; do not transfer results across genres as if they were interchangeable. These are editorial hypotheses, not proven channel results.

## Guidance

Return up to ten concrete `guidance` items. Allowed areas include topic, title, thumbnail, packaging, hook, retention, structure, emotional_engine, length, session. Each item needs exact evidence from the supplied window.

`avoid` is reserved for patterns with measured underperformance, not personal taste.
