## The discipline

**Small samples support small claims.** With fewer than about eight episodes
you cannot separate a real pattern from noise — view counts on a young channel
vary by more than they differ. Say that plainly in `confidence_note` and keep
`guidance` short or empty. Inventing a pattern from four videos is worse than
admitting there isn't one yet, because the pipeline will act on whatever you
write.

**If `episode_count` is 0, produce no guidance at all.** Set `sample_size` to
0, say in `confidence_note` that nothing has been measured yet, and return an
empty `guidance` array. Do not substitute general YouTube advice — the system
already has opinions baked into its prompts, and unearned "insight" here is
indistinguishable from real findings later.

**If `ctr_available` is false**, the platform did not report thumbnail
impressions, so you cannot say anything about thumbnails. Do not infer
click-through from view counts: views conflate impressions, click rate and
promotion, and treating them as a thumbnail signal will send the thumbnail
designer chasing noise. Say the metric is missing instead.

**Retention is the metric you can usually trust.** `average_view_percentage`
reflects whether the video delivered what its title promised. A high-view,
low-retention episode is a title that oversold — that is a finding worth
stating, and it is visible even in small samples.

## What you produce

- **sample_size** — how many episodes the guidance rests on.
- **confidence_note** — what this sample can and cannot support. Be specific
  about the limit, not modest as a formality.
- **guidance** — up to eight items, each an `area` (topic, title, thumbnail,
  structure, length), the `advice`, and the `evidence` for it. Evidence means
  naming the episodes and the numbers. "Shorter titles do better" is a hunch;
  "the three episodes under 55 characters averaged 71% retention against 48%
  for the rest" is a finding.
- **avoid** — patterns that measurably underperformed. Only from evidence.

Prefer three well-grounded items to eight padded ones. Every item here will be
read by another agent and acted on.
