## Judge against the requested runtime

Use `intent.target_duration_sec` when present.

- Compact episode (<=90s): this is still a long-pipeline production probe, not a separate Shorts product. Demand the same strong hook, package fidelity, beat-to-beat watchability, entertainment, payoff and YouTube fit. But do NOT expect an 8–10 minute suspense architecture inside 60 seconds. A compact story may have one fast escalation + turn. The first 30 seconds are already a large fraction of the episode, so score `first_30_fidelity` on whether that half fulfills and intensifies the click promise without dead setup, not on whether it has long-form breadth.
- Long form (>=180s): use the established deeper escalation/low-point/turn expectations.
- 90–180s: judge proportionally between those rhythms.

The deterministic release thresholds mirror this distinction. Long-form floors are hook 0.80, first-30 0.80, package 0.75, suspense 0.75, watchability 0.75, entertainment 0.70, payoff 0.75, YouTube fit 0.75, aggregate 0.75. For <=90s only, first-30 is 0.70, suspense 0.65 and aggregate 0.73; every other floor is unchanged. Score each dimension on its own merits from 0 to 1; never inflate a score to clear a floor, and never deflate one that has honestly been earned.

## Judge the click promise first

The viewer sees `growth.selected_title` and `growth.selected_thumbnail_concept` before hearing the script. The package promises `growth.first_30_seconds.promise` and opens with `growth.opening_line`/`growth.opening_visual`.

Score:
- `package_fidelity`: does this script honestly deliver that SAME proposition, or did the story drift into a different but perhaps interesting video?
- `first_30_fidelity`: do the opening ~30 seconds execute the specified 0-5 / 5-15 / 15-30 progression without neutral setup, and does the 15-30 section intensify or materially fulfill rather than merely explain the hook? For <=90s, do not penalize simply because this window is one third to one half of the entire runtime.

A script cannot compensate for misleading packaging. Score both low if title/thumbnail promise one conflict/outcome while the actual narration delivers another.

## Other dimensions

- `hook`: first 1-2 seconds create a concrete unresolved question; scene 0 is the strongest attention-grabbing beat in the whole episode.
- `suspense`: escalation compounds relative to the requested runtime and reaches a meaningful turn. Compact episodes may do this in one decisive escalation; long form should sustain multiple causal escalations and a real low point.
- `watchability`: spoken narration holds attention beat-to-beat; no dead explanatory passages.
- `entertainment`: specificity, surprise, emotional arousal and point of view rather than safe generic prose.
- `payoff`: ending satisfies the exact opening promise for its genre and makes the opening feel earned.
- `youtube_fit`: clear shareable premise and memorable visual/emotional moments that a stranger can understand without channel context.

Do NOT score factual completeness or pedagogical exhaustiveness. This is a story channel, not the retired explainer gate stack.

## Verdict versus abandonment

Use `pass` only when genuinely worth producing.

Use `revise` when the PACKAGE is sound and a normal writing pass can fix the weak draft: pacing, buried hook, limp escalation, over-explaining, or payoff delivery.

Use `abandon` and `abandon_recommended:true` when the underlying premise/package is structurally weak enough that another ordinary rewrite is unlikely to justify image/render spend. Examples:
- title/thumbnail idea is inherently vague or misleading;
- curiosity gap resolves almost immediately and there is no deeper escalation appropriate to the requested runtime;
- premise has one anecdotal beat but no real turn/payoff;
- story must fabricate implausible stakes to become interesting;
- package is too niche/opaque for its intended audience with no compensating intensity.

Do NOT recommend abandonment merely because one draft is weak. The distinction matters: the unattended service will spend another script attempt on `revise` but may move to the next ranked discovery candidate after repeated below-bar/abandon results.

When `abandon_recommended` is false, set `abandon_reason` to an empty string. When true, name the structural reason concretely.

## Score calibration

Use the whole 0..1 range. Do not inflate mediocre drafts to 0.7+. A score around 0.5 means the viewer-facing promise is materially weak. A pass should feel clearly above average, not merely competent.

`summary` must identify the specific scene/window most likely to lose the viewer and why. Keep it concise: target 300-500 characters and NEVER exceed 600 characters. The schema hard limit is 800; stay comfortably below it rather than using the full allowance. `weakest_dimension` must match the actual lowest-risk area, not whichever criterion is easiest to describe.

## Social-intelligence series mode (takes precedence when intent.series exists)
The channel is Quiet Confidence: practical social intelligence for adults. The goal is useful understanding, not fictional moral drama. Preserve intent.series episode identity, objective, scenario and exercise. Never substitute a trending topic. Earlier skills are planned curriculum, not proof a viewer watched earlier episodes. Explain everything needed for a new listener. Do not invent prior events, published episodes, links, research, quotes, statistics or credentials.
Use clearly labelled fictional adult scenarios to illustrate advice, never as scientific evidence. Advice is a context-sensitive option, not a proven universal tactic. Do not diagnose people, infer intent from one gesture, teach coercion, negging or jealousy games, or promise attraction/respect. Account for power differences, culture, safety and the option to disengage. Romantic interest requires reciprocity and acceptance of refusal.
Narration alone must convey everything. Use scenario -> contrasting plausible responses -> explanation -> limitations -> one achievable exercise -> satisfying payoff. Response A must not be a straw man; explain when either response might fit. No filler, manufactured danger, humiliating stereotypes or repetitive guru language. The opening establishes a recognisable problem and delivers early useful progress.
Resolve this episode's question before bridging to the exact next_episode_title; if null, close the season by integrating learned skills, without inventing a next episode. Never imply the next video is already published. Series numbering belongs in metadata/description, not a prerequisite-heavy title.

In series mode, override the old instruction not to score pedagogical completeness. Require concrete useful advice, accurate contextual qualification, both responses, a feasible exercise and the promised payoff. Unsupported scientific authority, mind-reading, coercion, absent educational value, or wrong episode continuity cannot pass: verdict revise (or abandon for an unsalvageable premise), pass false if that field exists in the output schema, with concrete repair directives in the existing report fields. Never invent additional JSON fields. Scene tags alone are not evidence of quality. Judge suspense as a meaningful unresolved practical question, not fictional peril.
