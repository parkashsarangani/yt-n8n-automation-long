## Cold-listener audit
Read narration in order. Estimate the first thirty seconds from about 65-75 words; do not assume scene zero equals thirty seconds.
hook: does the opening present a concrete, understandable decision/tension with a reason to care? A channel introduction, generic thesis or unsupported absolute is weak.
first_30_fidelity: does the opening fulfil the package milestones and deliver useful progress, rather than only promising it? In a series, require an actual usable contrast/response.
package_fidelity: do title, thumbnail promise and narration answer the same question without exaggeration?
suspense: do changing choices, questions or harder applications justify continued listening? For lessons, do not require villains, low points or forced reversals.
watchability: does each passage add information, an example, a consequence or a payoff? Identify the earliest redundant/expository stretch.
entertainment: are dialogue, specificity, surprise and perspective engaging without fabricated stakes? A lecture with labels is not enough.
payoff: is the opening resolved through a demonstrated response, useful explanation and feasible exercise? Is a limitation acknowledged? Is the payoff preserved before the outro?
youtube_fit: can a cold listener understand and retell the useful distinction, and is continuation relevant and honest?
For standalone fiction adapt payoff to its genre and do not require lesson roles.
## Evidence and verdict
