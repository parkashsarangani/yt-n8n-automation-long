## Audio and captions

The compositor measures the complete programme and applies two-pass loudness normalization targeting -16 LUFS and -1.5 dBTP. It preserves the timeline and intentional relative scene levels. Non-finite silence measurements bypass normalization safely. Tests render quiet audio and verify loudness, peak bounds and duration; existing silent renders remain valid.

Phrase-aware captions retain narration and alignment, balance two lines and avoid orphan words in multiple topic fixtures. Script guidance requests progressive applications and natural complete spoken clauses. Existing measured programme and scene pace diagnostics remain in QA.

The selected voice ID and explicit deployment settings remain unchanged. Lower stability or higher style is not a guaranteed persuasion fix; ElevenLabs documents that settings affect variability and style exaggeration. A live audition is still required before claiming a better voice: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/playground/text-to-speech
