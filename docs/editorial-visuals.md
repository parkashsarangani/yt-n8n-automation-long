# Editorial presentation

New episode renders use native typography instead of generating a generic background illustration. Cached generated backgrounds are not revived on retries. Quote excerpts, response contrasts and individual actions use different layouts, with restrained reveals timed to narration. Persistent scene counters, repeated headings and the full-height brand stripe are removed from video; measured publishing chapters remain available.

Hybrid mode adds short establishing shots from a reviewed local footage library, then returns to the scene's typography. It selects up to four distinct relevant assets, preserves framing and captions, and ignores clip audio. Comparisons and exercises remain graphics. The episode-linked thumbnail uses a frame from the first selected asset, with the existing OCR screen and clean typography fallback.

Writer and critic instructions favor specific dialogue, ordinary phrasing and concise excerpts. They prohibit invented experience as a substitute for natural delivery. See media-library/README.md for supplying licensed clips and photos. No arbitrary web images are fetched and no claim of human authorship is made.

Set FOOTAGE_MODE=hybrid after populating the mounted media-library folder. Existing installations retain graphics mode until configured. Once hybrid is enabled, missing or irrelevant assets are explicit errors, not a silent text-only fallback. Credits survive into the render artifact and YouTube description. A manifest records the operator's rights and suitability review; it is not an automated legal or aesthetic judgment.

Use the reproducible visual-smoke render to inspect layout and timing. Production speech still needs listening review; synthetic test speech is not evidence of natural vocal performance. Compare actual retention and engagement after release.
